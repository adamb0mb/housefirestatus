    What this tool does

    • Geocodes the address you enter (US Census Bureau geocoder, with an OpenStreetMap fallback).
    • Checks it against live, official evacuation zone data from Spokane & Stevens County emergency management, and current fire perimeters from the National Interagency Fire Center (NIFC).
    • Checks Avista power outages and TDS internet outages live, straight from each provider's own public outage data. Comcast, Spectrum, Quantum Fiber, Ziply Fiber, and gas don't publish an equivalent public feed, so those link directly to the provider's own status checker instead.

    Built for people displaced by the 2026 Spokane Complex wildfires (Old Trails, Autumn Lane, and Fairview fires). Data can lag reality during a fast-moving incident — always double check with 911, InciWeb, or Spokane County before making decisions.

    Not an official emergency service. Data sources: US Census Geocoder, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, NIFC WFIGS, Spokane & Stevens County GIS, Washington DNR.
